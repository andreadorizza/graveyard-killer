# Graveyard Killer

Your saved posts are a graveyard. Share a post from your phone to your AI agent, and Graveyard Killer files what it says in your own Library (Notion, Google Sheets or markdown). It brings the posts back to you in a weekly Digest. It needs no server and no API keys.

**Status: design stage.** Nothing is ready to install yet.
