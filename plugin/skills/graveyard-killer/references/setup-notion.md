# Setup in Notion

The Notion steps of [setup.md](setup.md). Read [library-notion.md](library-notion.md) first.

## Create the Library

Check that your Notion connector works, for example by reading the user's Notion profile or searching. If it does not, ask the user to connect Notion first. Ask in one question what to call the Library, suggesting "Graveyard Killer" as a private page. Then create it exactly as [library-notion.md](library-notion.md#layout) describes:

- the private page with that name;
- the Entries database inside it, with every field in the user's language, the six Statuses and the Category options;
- the `Settings` page, with every section. The Limits start at 20 per day and 100 per week, the Instagram login with "Browser" empty and "Reads per run" at 3, and the Version is the newest heading in [upgrade.md](upgrade.md#steps). The Schedule and "Ping by" stay empty until Setup's steps 3 and 9;
- the Cover property and the views, as [Add the views](library-notion.md#add-the-views) says.

Done when you can read the Settings back. Then tell the user in one line that their posts will show as cards with images in the gallery tab of Entries.

## Save from the phone

**Save through Notion.** This is the main way to save, with no tokens and with the computer off. In Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, the user taps Share, picks Notion, chooses the Library's Entries database as the destination, and saves. On an iPhone they first turn Notion on under the share sheet's More. Then check that a row with the link arrived in Entries. Notion says its share sheet supports only browsers and the photo roll; if the app is missing or says the app is not supported, use the next route.
